# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors must communicate respectfully, give actionable technical feedback,
and keep discussions focused on the project. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, doxxing, and publication of another person's private data are
not acceptable.

OncoPath handles sensitive health information. Never post real patient data,
credentials, private logs, or identifiable medical documents in issues, pull
requests, discussions, or test fixtures. Use synthetic data in every public
example.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior violates these standards. For ordinary moderation
concerns, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Security
vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted for this
project's medical-data privacy requirements.
